Data models

        
Documents (MongoDB-style filters
        $eq $ne $gt $gte $lt $lte $in $nin $regex $exists, $and/$or), key-value buckets with TTL,
        Redis-style lists/sets/hashes, wide-column families, HNSW vector search (experimental).
        Secondary field indexes via the Indexes panel.

        
Safety

        
Destructive statements and actions always ask first and name their target. Console writes
        do not join transactions begun in the Transactions panel — that boundary is stated in the
        status bar, not hidden.
